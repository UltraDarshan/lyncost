# Privacy

Lyncost keeps everything you enter on your own computer. We (Wintershogun TechLabs) never receive,
see, or store your Lyncost data. There is no account, no analytics, and no advertising.

- **Your data:** `~/.local/share/com.lyncost.desktop/lyncost.db`, with backups in the `backups`
  folder next to it.
- **Network:** the app goes online only to check for a new version, by downloading a small version
  file (`version.json`) from GitHub. GitHub receives your IP address for that request, as for any
  download; no Lyncost data is sent.
- **Removing everything:** the uninstall script with `--purge` deletes the app and your data.

The full website privacy policy, which also covers downloads, is at
https://wintershogun.com/legal/privacy.
