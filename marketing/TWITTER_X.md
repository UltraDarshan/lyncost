**Tweet 1:**
> 6 months ago, I cancelled my cloud budgeting subscription and decided to build my own desktop accounting software.
> 
> Everyone said: "Nobody buys desktop software anymore. Build an AI SaaS."
> 
> Here is why they were wrong, and why desktop-first software is having a massive renaissance. 🧵👇

**Tweet 2:**
> People are experiencing severe "subscription fatigue."
> 
> Paying $10/mo for note apps, $15/mo for to-do lists, and $12/mo for budget trackers is burning users out. When you cancel, you lose access to your own personal history.
> 
> People miss the era of "buy once, own forever."

**Tweet 3:**
> Second: privacy.
> 
> Financial aggregators get breached or quietly change terms to train models on purchasing habits. A local SQLite database sitting on your SSD cannot be leaked in an AWS bucket misconfiguration.

**Tweet 4:**
> Third: performance.
> 
> We’re tired of software that takes 4 seconds to open a window because it has to boot an entire Chromium instance.
> 
> Writing in Rust + Tauri v2 let us ship a 4.1 MB binary that launches before your finger lifts off the mouse.
