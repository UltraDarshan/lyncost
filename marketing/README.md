## 🎯 Positioning & Core Value Propositions

### Tagline
> **Private personal finance. Built with Rust, Tauri, and SQLite.**

### Elevators
- **One-Liner (10 words):**  
  Fast, private personal finance app that never connects to the cloud.
- **Short Pitch (25 words):**  
  A lightweight 4.1 MB desktop finance app for Windows and Linux that tracks spending, investments, and runway locally with zero telemetry and no monthly fees.
- **Full Pitch (50 words):**  
  Lyncost is an air-gapped personal accounting app engineered with Rust, Tauri v2, and local SQLite. It runs under 30 MB of RAM, launches instantaneously, and stores every transaction directly on your hard drive. No bank passwords required, zero background trackers, and one-time ownership for life.

---

## ⚡ The 4 Pillars to Emphasize in Marketing

| Pillar | What to Highlight | Proof Point |
|---|---|---|
| **Privacy First** | Never connects to your bank, zero analytics, air-gapped local drive | Local SQLite database, no third-party SDKs, open GitHub release checks only |
| **Engineered Performance** | Instant launch, ultra-lightweight, battery friendly | 4.1 MB binary size, <30 MB RAM usage (vs 500 MB+ Electron/cloud apps) |
| **No Subscriptions** | Pay once or download native Linux packages; zero recurring fees | 14-day free trial on Windows, lifetime key at ₹799 / $9.99; native packages for Linux |
| **Actionable Cashflow** | Predicts safe-to-spend runway rather than just looking at past expenses | 30-day projection blending scheduled bills, recurring rules, and current liquid cash |
